# Manual operator pilot

Not yet run. No operator identities or endorsements have been invented.

## Recruitment
Pilot owner (unassigned) recruits two or three operators covering Norway and/or Iceland. Confirm consent, data-processing responsibilities and that this is a fitness preparation tool, not mountain-safety clearance. Record participant identity only in the approved private system, not this repository.

## Sessions
1. Operator registers through the managed email provider and creates an owner-only company workspace.
2. Operator checks a familiar trail at all three training levels. Inspect insufficient time, narrow runway and comfortable runway.
3. Operator reviews crux, reward, exposure and typical season against their expertise. Disagreement blocks content sign-off.
4. Operator discovers and compares trails, including those too soon. Confirm none disappear until filtered.
5. Operator views the full plan without signing in, saves it after login, logs a session, reloads and verifies it remains completed.
6. Use a second isolated account and confirm no other participant's workspace or plan appears.
7. Review a missed-week scenario and the explicit distinction between logged progress and actual fitness.
8. Review a date outside forecast coverage and a provider outage. No stale current forecast should appear as trip-date weather.

## Record and decide
Track task completion, misunderstood wording, trust concerns, content corrections, save return rate and willingness to pay. Do not record medical details or raw chat transcripts. Define success targets with the pilot owner before sessions.
